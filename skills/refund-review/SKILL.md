# Refund review

1. Read the order outcome and applicable return-policy facts.
2. Assess eligibility without approving, issuing, or promising a refund.
3. Explain the decision factors and the next human-approved step.
4. Keep personal and payment data out of the response.
5. Record `skill_name=refund-review`, the skill version, and the policy decision in the trace.
